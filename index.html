About Meld

Meld is a personal planner and budget app for iPhone and Mac, built by an independent developer.

  • Today: tasks, events and routines for the day.
  • Focus: timers and a record of the time you invest in what matters.
  • Finance: expenses, income, accounts and budgets. Meld can import transactions from your own bank accounts, read-only, so you only have to review and categorize them.
  • Projects: long-term goals that fill up as you go.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Your records stay with you

Everything you record in Meld (tasks, events, focus sessions, transactions, budgets and projects) is stored on your own devices. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also stored in your private iCloud account so your iPhone and Mac stay in step. The developer has no server that holds your records and cannot see them.

Bank connections

If you choose to connect a bank account, Meld uses Plaid to read your account details and transaction history. The connection is read-only: Meld cannot move money, make payments or change anything at your bank. You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window; Meld never sees or stores your bank username or password. Imported transactions appear in Meld for you to review before they are added. You can disconnect a bank at any time, and Meld stops reading from it.

AI assistants (optional)

Meld can work with an AI assistant you set up yourself. When you turn one on, the parts of your schedule and records you allow in Meld's Agent access settings are sent to that assistant so it can answer and suggest plans. Any change it suggests is shown to you first and only takes effect when you accept it. Nothing is shared with an assistant unless you turn it on.

What Meld does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • Your data is never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

You can delete any record in the app. Deleting Meld from your devices removes its data from them, and you can remove Meld's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.
